# Orbit platform overview

Orbit is a fictional inference platform used as the agent evaluation corpus for InferScale-X.
Orbit was first released in March 2021 by the Halcyon Systems team.

# Pricing

The Orbit Standard plan costs 40 dollars per seat per month. The Orbit Pro plan costs 95 dollars per seat per month.
Annual billing gives a 15 percent discount on either plan.

# Limits

Each Orbit workspace may run at most 12 concurrent deployments.
The default request timeout is 90 seconds and can be raised to 300 seconds on the Pro plan.
