# Orbit regions

Orbit runs in three regions: Lisbon (eu-west-orbit), Osaka (ap-east-orbit) and Denver (us-central-orbit).
The Denver region has the largest GPU pool with 640 accelerators.
Lisbon is the default region for new workspaces created in Europe.

# Support

Orbit support responds within 4 business hours for Pro customers and within 2 business days for Standard customers.
The support escalation code word is TANGERINE.
